PILLAR 01 WSP MECHANICS
WHAT WSP ACTUALLY PENALIZES
Evaluates 3 discrete operational states per SKU-week:
  • Physical Stockout: Fixed 1.50 penalty (immediate loss of lifelong therapy).
  • Safety Floor Penetration (0 < DOH < SSD): Quadratic penalty (1 − DOH/SSD)² ∈ [0, 1.0]. Minor dips draw gentle penalties; approaching 0 accelerates quadratically.
  • Ceiling Overstock (DOH > Ceiling): Linear working capital and storage penalty capped at 1.0.
PILLAR 02 MATHEMATICAL PROOF
WHY THE N × 1.5 DENOMINATOR
Guarantees an invariant, scale-free percentage [0, 100%]:
  • Catastrophic Lower Bound: If all N = 260,000 SKU-weeks experienced physical stockout, Σ WSP = 1.5 × N. Denominator ensures CHI = 0%.
  • Theoretical Optimum: Zero corridor breaches across all weeks yields Σ WSP = 0 → CHI = 100%.
  • Comparative Standard: Enables unbiased cross-brand and cross-affiliate benchmarking across any planning horizon.
